# Project Instructions

## Mandatory Ponytail policy

Ponytail is permanently active in `full` mode for every prompt and conversation in this repository.

- Before planning, designing, coding, fixing, reviewing, or recommending an implementation, read and follow `.agents/skills/ponytail/SKILL.md`.
- Apply its ladder: question necessity, reuse existing code, prefer the standard library and native platform features, avoid new dependencies, and implement only the minimum that works.
- Do not add speculative abstractions, scaffolding, configuration, services, files, or features.
- Never simplify away validation at trust boundaries, data-loss prevention, security, accessibility, or requirements explicitly requested by the user.
- Keep this policy active throughout the conversation and after context compaction. Do not disable it unless the project owner explicitly changes this file.

## Product direction

Build a free, open-source, local-first music playlist organizer that is simple and intuitive for non-technical users. Favor sensible defaults and hide optional technical settings behind an advanced section. Avoid accounts, subscriptions, cloud services, telemetry, databases, and infrastructure unless a demonstrated requirement makes one necessary.
